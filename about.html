About Deals Dough Hub

We started with one idea: a good pizza shouldn’t cost a lot, and nobody should eat alone. So we bake a little more pizza and charge a little less.

Our story

Deals Dough Hub Pizza House is a neighborhood pizzeria built around sharing. Every dough ball is made fresh each morning, rested, stretched by hand and baked in a hot oven until the crust is golden and the cheese pulls.

Whether it’s a group deal for the whole barkada, a bowl of creamy pasta, or a solo pizza just for you, we make it the same way: fresh, generous and hot.
